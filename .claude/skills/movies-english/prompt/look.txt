LOOK AND FILMING STYLE

Photorealistic live-action romantic comedy, as if shot in the late 1990s on 35 mm colour negative film and printed: warm creamy skin tones, rich but soft colour (cream, camel, blush pink, burgundy, forest green, navy), gently lifted blacks, rolled-off highlights with soft halation around windows and practical lights, fine natural film grain, slightly soft lens, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh. Glamorous and cinematic like a polished Hollywood rom-com still, never a digital or CGI look: no HDR, no airbrushed or plastic skin, no over-sharpening, no vignette, no VHS lines, no light leaks, no sepia.

Vertical 9:16 frame composed for a phone: the speaking face sits in the upper half, large and clear; the lower third stays calm (body, counter, table, snow) so captions can sit there.

BRIGHT, AIRY, HIGH-KEY: soft diffused light fills the whole frame, faces evenly and flatteringly lit, low contrast, milky lifted shadows, a pale creamy-pastel palette (ivory, cream, blush pink, camel, soft white snow). Night, rain or candlelit scenes are still bright and warm like a well-lit 1990s studio rom-com set: never dark, never moody, never teal-and-orange, never deep black shadows. Natural motivated light: window daylight, overcast daylight, warm lamps, string lights, snow light. Real lived-in, beautiful places with tasteful detail. Signs and papers carry no readable words; background text stays out of focus.

Camera: steady, unhurried, eye-level, a locked shot or one slow motivated push-in. Never handheld shake, whip pans, rapid zooms or slow motion. One continuous take: no cuts inside the clip.

Performance: playful, charming, restrained film acting: small knowing smiles, eye rolls, a raised brow, timing in the eyes. Only the named speaker moves their mouth; anyone listening keeps their lips closed and reacts with the eyes.

Faces: the cast are young and very attractive in a natural, unretouched way, with natural skin texture and the specific features described. Nobody resembles any real actor, celebrity or public figure. Every person looks exactly the age given.

NO TEXT ON SCREEN: no titles, captions, subtitles, logos or watermarks. LEFT and RIGHT in a face description mean the person's own left and right.

LOOK AND FILMING STYLE

Photorealistic live-action romantic comedy, a frame from a glossy late-1990s Hollywood studio film shot on 35 mm Kodak colour negative with a soft Pro-Mist diffusion filter: warm, gently muted golden colour (nothing oversaturated), glowing creamy skin, warm cream whites, rich deep warm-brown shadows (dark, not grey), highlights that bloom softly around windows, lamps and fairy lights, fine film grain, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh. Luxurious, romantic and glamorous. Never a cold, grey, blue, green or teal tint, never flat overcast light, never a digital or HDR look, no vignette, no VHS lines, no light leaks, no sepia.

Vertical 9:16 frame composed for a phone: the speaking face sits in the upper half, large and clear; the lower third stays calm (body, counter, table, snow) so captions can sit there.

Light: warm, flattering, beautiful: soft warm daylight, sunlight through shop windows, snow light, warm tungsten lamps, fireplace and Christmas-light glow. Faces are always well and softly lit from the front, glowing. Night, rain or snow scenes stay warm and glamorous, lit by warm practical lights.

Camera: steady, unhurried, eye-level, a locked shot or one slow motivated push-in. Never handheld shake, whip pans, rapid zooms or slow motion. One continuous take: no cuts inside the clip.

Performance: playful, charming, restrained film acting: small knowing smiles, eye rolls, a raised brow, timing in the eyes. Only the named speaker moves their mouth; anyone listening keeps their lips closed and reacts with the eyes.

Faces: the cast are young (20-23) and breathtakingly beautiful, like elegant 1990s supermodels in a glossy rom-com: the women in polished full glam makeup and big glossy blowout hair, the men clean-cut and model-handsome. Soft-focus flattering skin with a warm glow, never waxy or plastic. Poised, confident expressions with small knowing half-smiles; even annoyance reads as a cool raised brow, never a frown, a worried brow or a wrinkled forehead. Nobody resembles any real actor, celebrity or public figure. Every person looks exactly the age given.

NO TEXT ON SCREEN: no titles, captions, subtitles, logos or watermarks. LEFT and RIGHT in a face description mean the person's own left and right.

LOOK AND FILMING STYLE (match references/reference-study.md)

Photorealistic live-action romantic comedy, a frame from a late-1990s Hollywood rom-com as it looks on a DVD: bright, soft and airy, soft diffused front light, low contrast, gently muted warm pastel colour (cream, blush pink, camel, soft red, snow white), creamy whites, soft natural skin tones, a slightly soft picture with fine film grain and gentle halation around bright windows. Never a cold, grey, blue, green or teal tint, never deep crushed shadows, never saturated or HDR, never razor-sharp digital detail, no vignette, no VHS lines, no light leaks, no sepia.

Vertical 9:16 frame composed for a phone: the face sits in the upper half, large and clear; the area around three quarters down stays calm (body, counter, coat, snow) because a small subtitle sits there.

Light: soft, bright, flattering: daylight through big shop windows, bright snow light, soft warm lamps and Christmas lights. Faces evenly lit from the front, no hard shadows.

Camera: steady and unhurried, eye-level, a locked shot or a gentle motivated move. Never shaky, no whip pans, no slow motion. One continuous take: no cuts inside the clip.

Performance: real, candid comedic acting like a 1990s studio comedy: big genuine reactions when the moment calls for it (a wide grin, a laugh with teeth showing, open-mouthed shock, a scream), relaxed and natural between lines. Only the named speaker moves their mouth; anyone listening keeps their lips closed and reacts with the face.

Faces: the cast are very young (19-23) and extraordinarily beautiful like real young actors in a 1990s rom-com: the women polished glam (big smooth swept-back salon blowouts, soft liner, clean lipstick, matte brick-red allowed), fresh youthful faces with real skin texture; the men boyish and handsome. Never sculpted contour, airbrushed, waxy or plastic skin, never older than the age given. Nobody resembles any real actor, celebrity or public figure. Extras (bakers, skiers, passers-by, waiters) are ordinary people of all ages.

Focus: shot like an 85 mm lens wide open: only the faces in focus, the background melted into soft creamy blur. Soft focus with a Pro-Mist glow; real actors on a real set, never a render.

NO TEXT ON SCREEN: no titles, captions, subtitles, logos or watermarks. LEFT and RIGHT in a face description mean the person's own left and right.

# Growth plan: where and how to post Movies English

## Platforms, in order of effort → reach
1. **TikTok (primary)**: fastest discovery for new accounts; 15-30 s reels with a hook in 1 s fit
   its feed exactly. Vietnamese learners are a large, active audience there. Post 1-2 a day.
2. **Instagram Reels**: the reference reels live here; the 1990s rom-com aesthetic is what IG
   rewards (saves + shares). Same file, own caption. Use a Broadcast Channel for "phrase of the day".
3. **Facebook Reels + Page**: Vietnamese audience is huge on FB; reels get pushed to non-followers.
   Pin a weekly "10 phrases this week" carousel (the end cards) to the Page.
4. **YouTube Shorts**: slower start, long tail; later compile 10 episodes into a 5-min
   "Learn 10 phrases with movies" long video (monetisable, searchable).
5. Threads / X: optional, repost the card image with the phrase.

## What hooks viewers (from the references)
- First 1 s is a situation with tension (a flirt, a fall, a fight), never a greeting or a lesson.
- Gorgeous, recognisable recurring couples → viewers follow characters, not lessons. Keep the
  three pairs (Viv & Jamie, Sienna & Theo, Maya & Lucas) and give each a running storyline
  ("Part 2 tomorrow") so people subscribe to see what happens.
- Twist in the last 3 s → rewatch loop; the phrase card after it gives the save reason.
- Few words, big captions, key phrase in yellow, Vietnamese under it.

## Content mix per week (7-10 posts)
- 60 % romcom couple episodes (library batches 01-03, 06)
- 20 % idiom sight-gags (batch 09) — most shareable
- 20 % practical travel / work / small talk (04, 05, 10) — most saved
- Weekly: "Which phrase was it?" quiz reel built from 3 end cards; monthly "couple of the month" poll.

## Engagement levers
- `cta_vi` in every caption: ask viewers to answer in English in the comments; pin the best reply.
- Series titles: "Viv & Jamie #12" → playlists / IG Highlights per couple.
- Reply to comments with a new video (TikTok "reply with video") using the commenter's phrase.
- Post at 11:30-12:30 and 19:30-21:30 Vietnam time.
- Same file everywhere, captions per platform via the `social-captions` skill.

## Money later
- 10k followers: affiliate English courses/apps, brand integrations in scenes (cafe, travel).
- Long-form YouTube compilations + membership with PDF phrase books (the library already holds
  phrase, meaning and example for all 1000 videos).
- MusicGen music beds are CC-BY-NC: switch to ACE-Step or a licensed track before monetising.

# chinese-scenery-style

Paths are relative to this folder (`.claude/skills/chinese-scenery-style/`). Server, retries, shot history, QA and delivery come from `../muse-video/SKILL.md` ("Format skills" shared steps, "Travel videos" lessons); the AI music engine is `../muse-video/scripts/music.py`; the traveller is the recurring character of `../zoominview/` ("The character"). Work folder `~/Movies/museai/scenic-china-<slug>/`, final `scenic-china-<slug>.mp4` (the `scenic` prefix routes it to the Travel channel).

## The style (what every China video keeps)
Reference: the user's Shangri-La brief (2026-10-30), `stories/shangri-la.json`.
1. **Hook in the first 3 s**: shot 1 is the place's most distinctive landmark (its signature icon, e.g. Shangri-La's giant prayer-flag canopy or Songzanlin, never a generic lake or meadow), the most spectacular view of the place from frame 0 (no logo, no fade from black), the traveller close in the foreground, the camera revealing the landscape smoothly; the music's swell plays under it. Elegant text over it: `This is China.` / `Welcome to <place>.` (`styles.json` `hook_text`; a story may set its own `hook_text`, still true of the place). No other text in the video.
2. **A journey, not a montage**: 3-4 real locations of the place in a natural order, each introduced by a wide establishing shot, then the traveller in it, then one meaningful detail. Location changes and time passing are 0.6 s dissolves; shots inside a location are 0.2 s soft cuts on the downbeat (a one-frame cut between two moving shots reads as a jolt). The day moves forward (day → golden → sunset → bright early dusk, never night) and ends on her looking out, then a lingering wide view that gives the place the last moment.
3. **The traveller**: a beautiful young Vietnamese girl, slim and slender, always the same face as the reference (her eyes, nose, lips and hair, recognisably her) with a slim V-shaped jawline and defined cheekbones: `styles.json` `character.face` (`~/.local/share/museai/characters/china-girl/face.jpg`, the user's own photo, kept out of git; a sharp front selfie, cropped head and shoulders, EXIF orientation applied). Wardrobe: the region's local costume (`regions.<region>.traveller`, plateau = red Tibetan chuba with fur trim, striped cuffs and a white fur hat with a beaded headband), else the default red jacket and cream sweater; a story's `traveller` overrides any field (`face`, `look`, `wardrobe`). The same outfit in every shot. Mostly from behind, side-on or medium-wide; never a face close-up; relaxed real walking, no posing, nodding, waving or talking. Her hands are beautiful: slender, graceful, smooth skin, neat nails, five fingers (`styles.json` manner, `hands` shots).
4. **Camera and pace**: mostly wide / medium-wide, almost still: tripod shots where she walks into a still frame, or one very slow straight move (the frame drifts under a twentieth of its width per second; muse renders "slow" moves at 0.1-0.2 widths/s, which blurred the view in v5), calm air so grass and flags barely stir. Scenery is locked off or a slow straight push-in / pull-back / rise: never a pan or sideways glide across close foreground (Shangri-La grassland pan: the near grass smeared into blur) (tracking, gentle pan, slow push-in, glide back), real-time motion. No orbiting, repeated drone shots, rapid montage, abrupt zooms. Every shot lasts whole bars of the song (1 bar ≈ 2 s at 120 BPM): views 3 bars, people 2, details 1.
5. **Real China, its signature icons**: every story shows the place's icons people know it by (`styles.json` `regions.<region>.signature`, plus the place's own famous spots): Shangri-La without prayer flags, the prayer-flag canopy and the giant golden prayer wheel misses what makes it Shangri-La. Real landmarks, architecture, terrain and vegetation; one season and weather for the whole story (`season` in the story); religious places from visitor paths, no staged rituals or worshippers up close; no readable signs, logos or watermarks (`prompt/china.txt`).
6. **Real people, Chinese travel-film colour**: she must look filmed, not generated: clear even young skin with a natural fine texture, real hair and cloth, no beauty filter or doll face (`prompt/china.txt` REAL FOOTAGE). Colour is the clear, bright Chinese travel look (通透 / 清新): crisp air, clean vivid blue sky, warm sun on reds and golds, rich but natural colours, open shadows (`china.txt` LOOK), finished by `styles.json` `grade` (yellows/oranges and reds pulled down a quarter, slight gamma lift, soft S-curve, cool shadows, then a phone-camera finish: saturation and contrast down a little, a slight softness and fine grain instead of sharpening): a contrast + vibrance grade turned the sky inky and the grass orange (v5, rejected). A low-contrast lift looked washed out and flat (rejected 2026-10-30); no dark, heavy or moody grading, no night.
7. **Sound**: the song on top, catchy from the first second, soft during intimate moments, building to the final panorama; the clips' own location sound under it (breeze, water, footsteps, town). No narration, no dialogue.

## Music
Library: `~/.local/share/museai/music/chinese-scenery/` (outside the repo: audio stays out of git). `scripts/music_sections.py list` shows it.
- **A track the user gives** (file or video): `uv run --no-project scripts/music_sections.py add <file> --id <id> --tags "<genre, instruments, mood>"`, then add it to the region's `music` list in `styles.json` as `{"id": ..., "track": "<id>"}`. It prints the section map: bars, loudness, catchy score (loudness + repetition + punch) and role (hook / build / soft / outro).
- **Cut the window**: `music_sections.py cut <id> --seconds 40-60 --out <work>/track.m4a`. It opens on the one-bar pickup before a loud hook (the swell under the reveal) or on the hook itself, prefers a soft part past the first third (tea / street moments) and windows that run into the song's real ending instead of a fade. `--start S` forces it; read `show` and pick by ear when the story needs another arc. The window length decides the video length (story-driven: 40-60 s for 3-4 locations).
- **No track yet, or the user wants new music**: generate one in the region's style (`styles.json` `regions.<region>.music`: Tibetan plateau, karst, Jiangnan water town, imperial, Silk Road, south, city; sung Chinese lyrics with `{place_zh}` or instrumental):
  ```bash
  uv run --no-project ../muse-video/scripts/music.py --style "<tags>" --bpm <bpm> --body-beats 128 \
    [--lyrics "<lyrics with {place_zh} filled in>"] --out <work>/song.m4a
  uv run --no-project scripts/music_sections.py add <work>/song.m4a --id <region>-<slug> --tags "<tags>"
  ```
  then cut it like a user track. ACE-Step sings (installed); MusicGen (fallback) is instrumental only. Rotate: do not reuse the same generated song for two places.
- Shangri-La (2026-10-30): the user's song (`shangri-la-ref`, 123 BPM, 182 s): window 126.71-182.29 s = pickup swell → loud chorus (lake, grassland, monastery) → soft bar at 35 s (teahouse) → big build at 41 s (dusk view) → the song's real ending under the final panorama.

## Steps
0. **Earlier shots**: `python3 ../shot-history/scripts/shots.py check --place "<place>" --country China` (shared step 1); `ALREADY SHOT` → new locations / angles, ask before rendering.
1. **Story** (`stories/<slug>.json`, copy `shangri-la.json`): `place`, `place_zh`, `where` (place, prefecture, province, altitude), `region` (a `styles.json` region), `music` (a region music id), `season` (one coherent season, the real vegetation, water level, sky), and `shots` in order, each with `bars` (last shot `0` = rest of the track), `person` (`none` `back` `walk` `side` `hands`), `light` (`day` `golden` `sunset` `dusk` `blue-hour` `mist` `overcast`; dusk and blue hour are written bright and early), `view` (the real landmark and what is really there, named), `action`, `move`, `ambience`, `in` (`dissolve` for a new location or time passing). A signature landmark muse keeps getting wrong gets `ref`: `{"image": "~/.local/share/museai/places/<slug>/<x>.jpg", "what": "..."}`, a real photo of it (the user's own, kept out of git) attached instead of the face; a photo with the traveller in it carries her face too. Follow the style above: wow hook, 3-4 locations, establishing → traveller → detail, intimate shots on the song's soft part, final look-out + lingering wide.
2. **Music**: cut the window (above) into the work folder.
3. **Plan**: `python3 scripts/plan.py stories/<slug>.json --work ~/Movies/museai/scenic-china-<slug>` → `plan.json` (each shot's start/end on the song's bars, render length) and `shot<N>.txt` prompts (place, shot, traveller with face reference, `prompt/china.txt`, the travel block without its music line). It fails when a shot gets under 1.6 s: fewer bars or a longer window.
4. **Render** (one background call; the shots one at a time through `render-queue`): `scripts/render ~/Movies/museai/scenic-china-<slug>/plan.json [N ...]`. Upstream 502/503 or a failed submit → re-run the failed numbers later.
5. **Check each shot** (shared step 5): `python3 ../muse-video/scripts/qa.py shotN.mp4 --travel 0 sheetN.jpg` plus the sheet: the real landmark (golden roofs, real street, real lake), the same jacket and face, five-finger hands, no text, no invented buildings, the planned light. Re-render only failing shots (`render plan.json 3 7`).
6. **Compose**: `uv run --no-project scripts/compose.py ~/Movies/museai/scenic-china-<slug>/plan.json [--at N=S]` → `scenic-china-<slug>.mp4`, then `qa.py <final> --travel <cuts>` and a contact sheet (`ffmpeg -i <final> -vf "fps=1,scale=180:-1,tile=8x7" -frames:v 1 sheet.png`): frame 0 shows the view with the readable hook text, every cut on a downbeat, the last view lingers.
7. **Deliver** (shared step 7): thumbnail (`../thumbnail/`, style `travel`, the hook shot or the final panorama, `--title "<PLACE>"`, `--sub "China 🇨🇳"`), captions (`../social-captions/`, first line a hook such as "This is China 🇨🇳 Welcome to Shangri-La"), shot-history record (`--skill chinese-scenery-style --template <region>`) before `FINAL=1`.

## Lessons (Shangri-La, 2026-10-30)
- `qa.py` `motion` TIME-LAPSE on tracking / pans / push-ins is the planned move: judge from the sheet. Clip `sound` FAIL does not matter (the song replaces it).
- A `hands` shot holds the hands only ~1.5 s, then muse pulls back to her face at the window, even with "locked, top edge below her chest" in the story (two takes). Compose it with `--at N=0` (hands open the shot, the pull-back reads as a reveal) and give it 1-2 bars; for a longer pure detail, shoot the cup without her (`person` `none`).
- `internal_error` / failed submit on a few shots of a parallel batch: re-run only those numbers; they pass on the second try.
- "Skin with fine pores and tiny imperfections" made muse paint her face with dark freckles (hook close-up, user rejected): ask for clear, even skin with a natural fine texture and name "no freckles, no spots"; texture comes from the camera, not from marks.
- Wind-whipped tall grass smears into blur even on a locked camera, and slow-motion interpolation (minterpolate) only warps it: ask for short grazed turf, calm air, a raised viewpoint.
- The prayer-flag canopy (user's reference clip): seen from inside it is a round tent of flags in concentric rings like a giant umbrella, mostly white / pale blue with red, yellow, green, blue rings, and a neat tiered cone of layered flags at the pole's foot; without that wording muse piles a messy heap of scarves there (rejected). The giant golden prayer wheel of Guishan Park, glowing at dusk, is Shangri-La's other icon: give it its own shot.
- Zhangjiajie v1 ("cinema camera", polished light, her close in the foreground) looked AI-generated to the user. v2 was accepted: the prompt asks for a visitor's unedited smartphone clip (soft phone detail, sensor noise, flat colours, the place's real haze), every person shot puts her among real Chinese tourists at railings and on paths taking phone photos (`REALISM` line in `plan.py`), the camera is "phone held still", and the grade adds softness and grain. The crowds did the most: an empty viewpoint with one pretty girl reads as staged.
- Faces read as AI with "cinema camera, 35 mm": asking for a real visitor's phone travel clip (Douyin look), her own warm skin tone (not porcelain), no rim light, and grain instead of sharpening in the grade made her look filmed (v7). "Petite, narrow shoulders, robe belted tight at a small waist" keeps the costume from making her look wide.
- The canopy from words alone stayed wrong three times; the user's photo as `ref` got it right first time.
- Face drift: the user's own face photo gave freckled, dull skin in some shots while one shot (prayer-wheel railing) came out exactly right. Once a shot nails her face and costume, crop that frame (head and shoulders, out of git next to the face photo) and set it as the story's `traveller.face`, then re-render the other person shots: one rendered face carries over better than the words or the original photo.
- Shangri-La (Chinese viewer, 2026-10-30): 16 s of lake and grassland before the prayer-flag canopy lost viewers; open on the signature landmark and keep generic scenery short and later. The grade's red/yellow desaturation and "mostly white and pale blue" canopy wording made the flags look faded: name the five saturated flag colours and keep the grade's saturation at or above 1. The costume read as generic until it carried the local jewellery and makeup (coral, turquoise, amber beads; rosy plateau blush, red lips): name both in the region wardrobe.
- The hat never matched across shots: each shot is a separate render and a hat in words came out as a fluffy ring, beret, short-pile cap, red-crowned hat or open fur band ("fox fur" grew ears; "white" went cream), and the user switched between styles. The fix was dropping it: the local festive look the user chose (`look-songzanlin.webp` in the private place folder) is no hat, a forehead ornament on a centre parting and two long threaded braids; hair and a fitted robe stay the same in every shot. Prefer a hatless wardrobe; if a hat is a must, describe one shape only and keep it.
- A face crop cut from a rendered shot also shows that shot's clothes: set `traveller.ref_note` ("use only her face: no hat, not these clothes") when the wardrobe changed, or `traveller.ref_keeps` to copy something from it on purpose.
- The opening lost viewers once she was a small figure from behind (and a padded robe made her look heavy). The first 5 s are the hook: a 1-bar burst of the most colourful icon (looking up into the canopy), then her stepping up to the landmark (climbing the temple stairs, seen from behind, gimbal following); the user rejected a posed shot of her facing the camera with a smile, so `person` `front` is not for the hook; keep the robe "slim, closely fitted, never padded or boxy". Cut, don't dissolve, between mid-video shots of her.
- She read as AI in close faces and with festive makeup (rosy blush, red lips): "barely-there natural makeup, real skin texture, like an unedited phone video" fixed it. One shot per moment: a tea-hands close-up plus a teahouse shot was one tea shot too many (user).
- Costume drift: "a cream patterned blouse underneath" became white patterned sleeves, the hat vanished in a close shot, and a `ref` photo's own clothes leaked in. Name the sleeves' colour and material, "always wearing the hat, never bare-headed", and say a `ref` photo's clothes are not used.
- muse adds a backpack and draws golden grass as barley; name "no backpack" / "wild meadow grass" in the story when it matters.

GOWN-RUN REAL FOOTAGE REQUIREMENTS

NO TEXT ON SCREEN. Must look FILMED, not generated: a real creator's unedited travel clip shot on a modern phone on a gimbal at a real place on a bright day. Bright, well-exposed image: open shadows, clean fresh daylight, soft sun breaking through white clouds with patches of blue sky, fresh vivid green grass, natural true colours like the place really looks on a nice day. Never dark, dim, gloomy, crushed or heavily graded; no vignette, no cinematic teal-orange look, no HDR halos, no beauty filter. Real textures with fine natural sensor grain, real wind.

THE WOMAN (the same woman in every shot): one slim, graceful young woman, about 25, very long thick wavy copper-red hair to her waist, real natural skin with fine texture. ALWAYS seen from behind or from behind and to one side; her face is never shown and never turned to the camera. Light, natural, unhurried movement at real speed; real hands with five fingers. She is alone: nobody else anywhere in the frame.

THE GOWN: exactly the gown the shot describes, same rich colour, cut and fabric in every frame, brightly lit so its colour glows against the green. Real fabric in real wind: capes, trains and skirts lift, billow and fall with weight; folds stay continuous; fabric never passes through her body, never melts, never changes colour or length.

CAMERA: one continuous real-time shot, never time-lapse, never slow-motion stutter. Follow shots glide smoothly behind her at hip height; wide shots are still or drift very slowly. Level horizon, no zoom, no spin, no shake.

THE PLACE: the real landmark, correct in shape and size, the same building in every shot, rigid and sharp; stone stays stone and never warps. It looks monumental next to her small figure in wide shots.

SOUND: only the real ambience: wind, fabric rustling, footsteps on grass, lapping loch water, birds. NO music, no voice, no speech, no singing.

Not a cartoon, animation, painting, game render or advertisement. No watermarks, no subtitles, no logos.

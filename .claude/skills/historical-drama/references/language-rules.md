# Language rules

## Dialogue
- Natural spoken Mandarin, Simplified Chinese. Ancient setting, modern learner-accessible speech.
- Use particles and reactions people really use: 嗯, 哦, 啊, 呀, 吧, 嘛, 哎呀, 真的吗, 然后呢, 怎么了.
- Interruptions, trailing off, follow-up questions, repeating to confirm - not full formal sentences.
- Few chengyu / classical lines; one occasional period flavour (称呼 like 公子, 姑娘, 老爷, 夫人,
  娘, 爹) is fine. Learner-useful forms stay modern (你好, 谢谢, 多少钱, 太贵了, 便宜一点).
- Register by character: elder (short, blunt, proverbs sparingly), child (simple, curious, questions),
  merchant (fast, bargaining idioms), scholar (precise, slightly formal), official (formal), friends
  (teasing). Each character keeps their own voice across episodes.
- Parents/children: real talk - nagging, deflecting, "我没事", "吃饭了吗", "你怎么才回来".

## Review block selection
- 10-15 words, 5-8 expressions per episode. Everyday-useful first (food, money, family, feelings,
  directions, health, help). Skip rare/literary words.
- Each taught item appears at least once in the episode dialogue, and is reused in the review dialogue.
- Mix: nouns, verbs, adjectives, measure words, a connector, a feeling word, a polite formula.
- Keep a running list in the ledger; don't re-teach the same item as new within 20 episodes (review
  episodes mark "ôn lại").
- Level ramp: season 1 = HSK1-2 core, then +~1 level per 2-3 seasons; always spoken-first.

## Accuracy checks (run before delivering)
- Pinyin: tone marks on the right vowel (a > e > o; in iu/ui the last vowel); tone-sandhi written as
  base tone (不 + 4th tone = bú is written bú: 不是 bú shì; 一 as yī/yí/yì per rule); neutral tone with
  no mark; ü as ü (nǚ, lǜ); apostrophe for ambiguity (Xī'ān, Wǎn'ér).
- Pinyin words spaced by word, names capitalised.
- Vietnamese gloss: natural Vietnamese (not Hán-Việt word-by-word); beware false friends (see
  `../hanviet-trap` for Hán-Việt traps).
- Example sentences: complete, spoken, short (5-12 hanzi), with pinyin + Vietnamese.
- Measure words correct (一碗面, 一件衣服, 一位客人, 一斤米).
- Numbers/prices: use ancient-flavour currency in the story (文, 两, 银子) but teach 多少钱 / 太贵了 /
  便宜点 patterns that transfer.

# Episode template (one episode per response, ~45 min)

Write all dialogue in Simplified Chinese with pinyin (tone marks) and a Vietnamese gloss under each
line in the script; scene directions in Vietnamese. Keep speaker names consistent with the bible.

```
# 第 N 集 · 标题 (pinyin) - Vietnamese title
Mùa X · Tập N/1000 · Mix: family | everyday | friendship | growth | romance/mystery
Continuity: threads continued · objects/clues in play · relationships at this point

## 1. Mở đầu kịch tính (0-2')
Cold open. First 5-15 s = the curiosity beat. Scene, speakers, dialogue. Ends with a question in the
viewer's mind; truth not fully revealed; true to the plot.
(No "hello, today we learn Chinese".)

## 2. Phát triển cốt truyện (2-12')
Continue last episode. Introduce people/situation through talk.

## 3. Hội thoại đời thường (12-30')
2-4 realistic scenes (ordering food, bargaining, visiting, doctor, apology...). Natural, interrupted,
follow-up questions. Humour + the episode's human experience begins here. Target words/expressions
appear organically.

## 4. Phát triển cảm xúc (30-38')
Relationships, funny beat, conflict, one moment that touches. Lesson through action/consequence.

## 5. Cliffhanger (38-41')
Twist or open question linked to a running thread.

## 6. Ôn tập tiếng Trung (41-45')
### 6.1 Từ vựng (10-15)
| # | 汉字 | pinyin | nghĩa tiếng Việt | ví dụ (汉字 + pinyin + Việt) |
### 6.2 Mẫu câu thực dụng (5-8)
| 汉字 | pinyin | nghĩa | khi nào dùng |
### 6.3 Cấu trúc câu quan trọng (1-3)
Pattern - rule in one line - 2 examples.
### 6.4 Hội thoại ôn lại (6-10 lines, uses the new words)
### 6.5 Quiz nghe hiểu (3-5 questions, answers given after)

## Ledger update (append to series/ledger.json)
- summary (3 lines) · new secrets · objects/clues · relationship changes · open threads ·
  vocabulary taught (for no-repeat and spaced review)
```

Length: a full script (5 story parts + review) is long; if it will not fit one response, deliver parts
1-3, then parts 4-6 on "continue". Never compress the review: the learner value lives there.

Spaced review: every ~10th episode re-uses 5-8 earlier words in new situations; mark them "ôn lại".

Season finale (episode 100 of a season): resolves the season storyline, pays off planted threads, one
emotional family payoff, one plant for the next season.

HEALING SLOW-LIVING FOOTAGE REQUIREMENTS

NO TEXT ON SCREEN. Ultra-photorealistic phone-camera footage of a real, lived-in country home, like a cosy slow-living vlog: soft natural light, warm gentle colours (golden green, cream, warm wood, terracotta, soft rose, sky blue), slight film softness, real dust and texture, nothing staged or glossy. Real vegetation, flowers and weather for this region and season only.

LIGHT AND BEAUTY: the most beautiful moment of the day for this place - warm low golden-hour sun (or soft early-morning light) falling across the scene, gentle backlight glowing through leaves, petals, steam and hair, long soft shadows, a clear sky with a few soft clouds, rich but natural colours (golden greens, honey light, soft blue sky). Clean, tidy, lovingly kept corners with flowers; a view that makes you want to breathe slowly. Never dull grey, dark or gloomy unless the shot asks for rain, and then the rain is soft with light still in the sky.

CAMERA: one short handheld shot held almost still, or one very slow gentle move (a slow push-in of a few centimetres, a slow sideways slide past a window frame or doorway). Level horizon, no zoom-out, no spinning, no fast pans, never dizzy. The shot is already calm in the first frame and stays calm to the last frame. Shallow natural depth of field on close details.

LIFE IN THE FRAME (real-time, never a time-lapse): something always moves gently and naturally - leaves and petals stirring in a light breeze, steam rising from a teacup or pot, a candle flame flickering, curtains breathing, raindrops running down glass, a river flowing, birds hopping. Buildings, furniture, tableware and window frames stay perfectly rigid and sharp; nothing warps, melts, shimmers or morphs. Objects stay where they are; nothing appears or disappears. Cut flowers in a vase, bowls, jars and cups never move by themselves: no flowers flying, growing or arranging themselves.

THE GIRL (only when the shot mentions her): one young woman, about 24, long chestnut-brown hair loosely tied with a few loose strands, cream knit cardigan over a linen dress, soft beige linen apron in the kitchen. She is seen from behind, from the side or as hands in close-up, unhurried and calm, real hands with five fingers, natural slow movements at real speed. She never looks into the camera and never speaks.

ANIMALS (only when the shot mentions one): a real animal of normal size and anatomy, relaxed, moving at its natural speed (a cat stretching, a dog resting, a bird pecking), never cartoonish, never doubled.

SOUND: only the real natural ambience of this place and moment, soft and close - rain on glass or leaves, birdsong, a flowing stream, wind in leaves, a crackling fire, the gentle clink of a spoon. NO music, no voice, no speech, no singing, no humming.

Natural lived-in realism, not a cartoon, animation, painting, advertisement or video-game render. No watermarks, no subtitles, no logos.

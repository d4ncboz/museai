HEALING SLOW-LIVING FOOTAGE REQUIREMENTS

NO TEXT ON SCREEN. Real, unstaged phone-camera footage of an ordinary lived-in home, like a bright, happy slow-living vlog shot on an iPhone on a sunny day: bright airy exposure, fresh clear natural colours (sunlit greens, white walls, warm wood, blue sky), fine sensor grain, real wear and texture. Real vegetation, flowers and weather for this region and season only.

REAL, NOT AI: it must look filmed, not generated. Plain rooms with only a few things in them: bare wood, a worn tea towel, a chipped bowl, crumbs, a slightly messy corner. Food is handmade and uneven, no two pieces alike. Light comes from ONE real source (a window, or one plain lamp), with the falloff and soft shadows a phone records; No orange glow, no HDR look, no glowing halos, no rim light, no candles, fairy lights or lamps unless the shot names one, never several at once. No props arranged for a photo, no flowers on every surface, no rows of identical objects. Colours are fresh and true: no saturated red-orange, no teal-and-orange grade, no grey wash.

LIGHT: bright and sunny. Sunshine streams in through the windows and lands in clear patches on the table, floor and walls; the room is light and airy, white walls bounce the light, shadows are soft and open, never murky. Outdoors a blue sky with a few white clouds. Never dim, never gloomy, never underexposed; no rain unless the shot asks for it, and then sun breaks through.

CAMERA: a static locked-off shot, completely still, level horizon. No camera, phone, tripod or filming gear anywhere in the frame. No push-in, no drift, no zoom, no pan; the framing in the last frame is the same as in the first. Natural phone depth of field: close details a little soft in the background, nothing artificially blurred.

LIFE IN THE FRAME (real-time, never a time-lapse): something always moves gently and naturally - leaves and petals stirring in a light breeze, steam rising from a teacup or pot, curtains breathing, raindrops running down glass, a river flowing, birds hopping. Buildings, furniture, tableware and window frames stay perfectly rigid and sharp; nothing warps, melts, shimmers or morphs. Objects stay where they are; nothing appears or disappears. Cut flowers in a vase, bowls, jars and cups never move by themselves: no flowers flying, growing or arranging themselves.

THE GIRL (only when the shot mentions her): one young woman, about 24, long chestnut-brown hair loosely tied with a few loose strands, cream knit cardigan over a linen dress, soft beige linen apron in the kitchen. She is seen from behind, from the side or as hands in close-up, unhurried and calm, real hands with five fingers, natural slow movements at real speed. She never looks into the camera and never speaks.

ANIMALS (only when the shot mentions one): a real animal of normal size and anatomy, relaxed, moving at its natural speed (a cat stretching, a dog resting, a bird pecking), never cartoonish, never doubled.

SOUND: only the real natural ambience of this place and moment, soft and close - rain on glass or leaves, birdsong, a flowing stream, wind in leaves, a crackling fire, the gentle clink of a spoon. NO music, no voice, no speech, no singing, no humming.

Natural lived-in realism, not a cartoon, animation, painting, advertisement or video-game render. No watermarks, no subtitles, no logos.

HEALING SLOW-LIVING FOOTAGE REQUIREMENTS

NO TEXT ON SCREEN. Real, unstaged phone-camera footage of an ordinary lived-in home, like a bright, happy slow-living vlog shot on an iPhone on a sunny day: bright airy exposure, fresh clear natural colours (sunlit greens, white walls, warm wood, blue sky), fine sensor grain, real wear and texture. Real vegetation, flowers and weather for this region and season only.

REAL, NOT AI: it must look filmed, not generated. Plain, CLEAN, tidy rooms with only a few things in them: scrubbed pale wood, a folded linen tea towel, one cream ceramic bowl, wiped worktops, nothing piled up, no clutter, no crumbs or mess, nothing lying around that the shot does not use (user 2026-11-10: the kitchen looked messy). Food is handmade and uneven, no two pieces alike. Light comes from ONE real source (a window, or one plain lamp), with the falloff and soft shadows a phone records; No orange glow, no HDR look, no glowing halos, no rim light, no candles, fairy lights or lamps unless the shot names one, never several at once. No props arranged for a photo, no flowers on every surface, no rows of identical objects. Colours are fresh and true: no saturated red-orange, no teal-and-orange grade, no grey wash.

LIGHT: bright and sunny. Sunshine streams in through the windows and lands in clear patches on the table, floor and walls; the room is light and airy, white walls bounce the light, shadows are soft and open, never murky. Outdoors a blue sky with a few white clouds. Never dim, never gloomy, never underexposed; no rain unless the shot asks for it, and then sun breaks through.

CAMERA: a static locked-off shot, completely still, level horizon. No camera, phone, tripod or filming gear anywhere in the frame. No push-in, no drift, no zoom, no pan; the framing in the last frame is the same as in the first. Natural phone depth of field: close details a little soft in the background, nothing artificially blurred.

LIFE IN THE FRAME (real-time, never a time-lapse): something always moves gently and naturally - leaves and petals stirring in a light breeze, steam rising from a teacup or pot, curtains breathing, raindrops running down glass, a river flowing, birds hopping. Buildings, furniture, tableware and window frames stay perfectly rigid and sharp; nothing warps, melts, shimmers or morphs. Objects stay where they are; nothing appears or disappears. Cut flowers in a vase, bowls, jars and cups never move by themselves: no flowers flying, growing or arranging themselves.

CONTAINERS AND VESSELS: fruit, berries, leaves or anything held in a colander, basket, bowl or jar stays inside it the whole shot; rinsing is gentle, nothing is thrown or tossed, nothing tumbles out or flies through the air. A teapot or kettle pours only with its lid already lifted off and set down beside it; a lidded vessel never pours. Exactly one kettle and one teapot in frame, never doubled.

THE GIRL (only when the shot mentions her; the SAME woman (face, hair, hands) in every shot of one video; her CLOTHES change from video to video, user 2026-11-13): one young woman, about 26, long chestnut-brown hair in the same loose low braid over her left shoulder with a few soft strands at the temples, wearing exactly the outfit the shot names (the same outfit in every shot of the video; if none is named, pick a colourful one, never the cream cardigan and white sweater), plus a soft linen apron in the kitchen. Never change the hair within a video. Her HANDS are beautiful and always the same: slender, smooth, young, soft pale-peach skin, long relaxed fingers, short clean rounded nails with a natural pale-pink polish, no rings, no wrinkles, no age spots, no scars, exactly five fingers, natural joints, graceful unhurried movements. She is seen from behind, from the side or as hands in close-up, calm, real speed. She never looks into the camera and never speaks.

ANIMALS (only when the shot mentions one): a real animal of normal size and anatomy, relaxed, moving at its natural speed (a cat stretching, a dog resting, a bird pecking), never cartoonish, never doubled.

SOUND: the real, close-miked sound of exactly what happens in the shot, clear and crisp, in sync with the action, as a phone records it from half a metre away - tea pouring into a cup, a knife crunching through a pie crust, butter sizzling in a pan, a cat's soft meow or purr, birds chirping in the garden, water running over fruit, dough slapped on a floured board, a spoon tapping a bowl - over the soft natural ambience of the place (birdsong, a stream, wind in leaves). NO music, no voice, no speech, no singing, no humming.

Natural lived-in realism, not a cartoon, animation, painting, advertisement or video-game render. No watermarks, no subtitles, no logos.

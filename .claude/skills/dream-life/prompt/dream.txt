DREAM-LIFE FOOTAGE REQUIREMENTS

NO TEXT ON SCREEN. One impossible, gentle moment from a dream life, filmed as if it were completely ordinary: the magic is treated as an everyday chore, calm and unhurried, never as a spectacle. The impossible thing is soft, real and tactile (fur, cotton, glass, porcelain, paper, light), with believable weight, texture and shadow, sharing the scene's light and colours.

COMPOSITION: vertical 9:16, a clean centred or symmetrical storybook frame, the subject in the middle third with clear sky or a simple background above it; one main subject, nothing cluttered at the edges. Pastel, harmonious colours from the look's palette only; no harsh neon, no pure black crushes, no orange-teal blockbuster grade.

CAMERA: one continuous shot, held still or one very slow gentle move (a slow push-in or a slow sideways glide of a few centimetres per second). Level horizon, no zoom-out, no shake, no spinning, no fast pans. Calm from the first frame to the last frame.

LIFE IN THE FRAME (real-time, never a time-lapse): exactly one gentle main action plus soft ambient motion - fabric and hair stirring in a breeze, clouds drifting, petals or snow falling, a glow slowly pulsing, steam rising. Movements are slow and graceful at natural speed. Houses, furniture, faces and hands stay rigid and well-formed; nothing warps, melts or morphs except the one magical thing the shot describes, and that changes slowly and smoothly.

THE CHARACTER (only when the shot names one): the same person, exactly as described, with natural proportions, real hands with five fingers, a calm content expression, dreamy and absorbed in the task. Seen in a three-quarter or side view, never staring into the lens, never speaking, never singing.

ANIMALS AND CREATURES: exactly one of each named creature, never doubled; giant or soft creatures keep friendly calm faces and move slowly, like a living plush toy.

SOUND: only soft natural ambience of the scene (breeze, birds, a distant chime, soft rain). NO music, no voice, no speech, no singing.

No watermarks, no subtitles, no logos, no signatures, no frames or borders.

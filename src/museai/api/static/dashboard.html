Every time here is Vietnam time (GMT+7): schedule it as shown. Each channel posts from its own accounts at its audience's peak hours, each platform at its own time for that kind of content; for an audience abroad its own clock is shown beside (“NY Mon 9:00 PM”). Chinese: learners' breaks and the study hour after dinner (YouTube Shorts in the evening only). Travel (English): the UK evening and the US East evening, which are night and morning in GMT+7. Dream life: two reels a day for US viewers, the US evening (8-9 AM in Vietnam) and the US morning commute (7-10 PM in Vietnam). Healing: one a day at the US evening. Each channel's queue fills its free slots back to back; use each platform's scheduler 1-2 days ahead; pick another slot on a card to change the order.

Each platform has its own caption, written to its length, hook and hashtag rules (tabs above the text; • = written for it; “Shared” is the fallback for a platform without one): pick the tab, “Copy” (YouTube: “Copy title” and “Copy description”), and “Show file” to drag the video in. Tick a platform after publishing; when all are ticked, the video moves to the Trash.
